# Stage 6: Launch

**Objective:** a practical launch plan the user can choose from, built on the accepted product.

## Steps

1. `factory.py start 6`.
2. Ask the user first (one question round) which parts they want: positioning and pricing, the platform's launch items, go-to-market channels, landing page, KPIs. Prepare only those.
3. Have `product-researcher` refresh competitor pricing and positioning with a quick scan; earlier research may be months old.
4. Brief `business-strategist` to write `docs/launch-plan.md` with the chosen parts:
   - positioning: who it is for, what it replaces, the one reason to choose it;
   - pricing hypotheses and the experiment that would test each;
   - acquisition channels ranked by cost to learn, each with a first small experiment and the result that would justify spending more;
   - the launch items in the platform file (store listings for mobile; domain, hosting, search visibility and notices for web);
   - landing page outline;
   - KPIs with precise definitions, the analytics event behind each, targets labeled as hypotheses;
   - launch checklist (accounts, legal pages, privacy disclosures, platform review requirements) and milestones;
   - what to build next under each plausible outcome.
5. Budgets separate verified costs (source and date) from estimates.
6. `factory.py complete 6`.

## Gate 5: launch

The user approves the plan and chooses what to execute. Spending money, publishing, submitting to stores and releasing anything publicly each need explicit authorization for that specific action; approving the document is not authorization to carry it out.
